# Bill Reminder XL — Organizer: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 14, 2026_

Bill Reminder XL stores all your bills and payment records locally on your device. Your data never leaves your iPhone.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Bill Reminder XL is a personal organizer. It does not connect to your bank, does not process payments, and does not give financial advice.

If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it.
