# Pocket Money: Allowance Log — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 12, 2026_

Pocket Money stores all records locally on your device. Names, balances, chores and goals never leave your iPhone.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts.

The app is made for parents. It does not ask for, verify, or store any real payment or banking information: it is a simple written record of cash pocket money.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

If you delete the app, all records are deleted with it.
