# DayLine — One Line Journal: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 19, 2026_

DayLine stores everything you write locally on your device. Your journal entries never leave your iPhone.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Optional daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device using the system notification service.

If you delete the app, your journal data is deleted with it. Use Export in Settings to keep a copy.
