# PrintExact: Print to Size — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 14, 2026_

PrintExact works entirely on your device. The photos you open, the sizes you type, and the projects you save never leave your iPhone.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts. It does not require an internet connection.

Photos: the app asks for access to your photo library only so you can pick a photo to print. Photos are used on your device to build the page you print or export. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Printing and export: printing goes straight from your iPhone to your printer through Apple's AirPrint. Files you export (PDF, JPEG, PNG) are saved where you choose in the Files app or shared by you.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

If you delete the app, your projects are deleted with it. Export your pages first if you want to keep them.
