# InkSize: Tattoo Stencil Maker. Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

InkSize does not collect any data about you.

Your pictures and stencils are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent anywhere. The app works without an internet connection.

Photos: when you pick a photo, iOS shows you its own picker and hands the app only the picture you chose. The app does not read your photo library. Pictures from the camera stay on your device.

Printing: when you print, iOS sends the stencil pages to the printer you choose. When you save a PDF or PNG, the file goes only where you send it.

The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details.

Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports if you allow that in iOS Settings.

If you delete the app, your stencils are deleted with it. iOS may include them in your device backup.
