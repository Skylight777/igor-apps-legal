# GLP-1 Log — Shot Tracker: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 3, 2026_

GLP-1 Log stores your injection diary, weight entries and side-effect notes locally on your device. Your data never leaves your iPhone.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts. It does not connect to the internet.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

GLP-1 Log is a personal diary. It does not calculate or recommend doses, does not interpret your weight or symptoms, and does not give medical advice. Your dose and schedule are decided by your prescriber. Medicine and brand names in the app are used only to label your own entries.

If you enable Face ID lock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS; the app never receives biometric data.

If you delete the app, your diary is deleted with it. Use Backup or Export in Settings to keep a copy.
