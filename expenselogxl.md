# Expense Log XL: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 30, 2026_

Expense Log XL stores all your expenses and categories locally on your device. Your financial data never leaves your iPhone.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no bank connections, and no user accounts.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it. Use CSV Export to keep a copy.
