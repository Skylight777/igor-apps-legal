# RowWise: Knitting Row Counter. Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

RowWise does not collect any data about you.

Your projects, pattern texts, notes and reminders are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent anywhere. The app works without an internet connection.

The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Lock Screen counter: if you turn it on, iOS shows your current row on the Lock Screen. This stays on your device.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details.

Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports if you allow that in iOS Settings.

If you delete the app, your projects are deleted with it. iOS may include them in your device backup.
