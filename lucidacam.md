# Lucida Cam: AR Drawing & Trace — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

Lucida Cam works entirely on your device. The pictures you choose, the projects you save and their settings never leave your iPhone unless you share them yourself.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts. It does not require an internet connection.

Camera: the app shows the live camera view under your picture so you can trace it onto paper. The live view is never recorded. If you use Take Photo, that photo is saved inside the app as your picture.

Photos and files: the app sees only the picture you pick. Nothing is saved to your photo library unless you choose Save Image in the share sheet when you share a grid picture.

The app is a paid download with no in-app purchases. Payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Apple may provide us with anonymous crash and usage reports if you allow that in iOS Settings.

If you delete the app, your projects are deleted with it. iOS may include them in your device backup.
