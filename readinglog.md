# Reading Log XL — Book Tracker: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 12, 2026_

Reading Log XL stores all your books and reading sessions locally on your device. Your reading diary never leaves your iPhone.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

PDF reports are created on your device and shared only when you choose to share them.

If you delete the app, your reading diary is deleted with it. Use the PDF report in the app to keep a copy.
