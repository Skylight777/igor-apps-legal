# Cholesterol Log XL: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 30, 2026_

Cholesterol Log XL stores all your lipid panel entries locally on your device. Your readings never leave your iPhone.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Cholesterol Log XL is a personal journal. It does not measure cholesterol, does not interpret your readings, and does not give medical advice. Always consult your doctor about your health.

Optional test reminders are scheduled locally using the system notification service. If you delete the app, your entries are deleted with it. Use Export in Settings to keep a copy.
